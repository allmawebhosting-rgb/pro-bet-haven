---
name: Free pick release schedule
description: Scheduling rule for the two free channel picks.
---

Free picks are delivered as one bundle per channel at that channel’s configured next-release time; they must not use independent release timestamps.

**Why:** The product promise is two free fixed matches arriving together at a specified time, while VIP picks can remain independently timed.

**How to apply:** When creating or editing a free prediction, use the channel’s shared release setting. When that setting changes, synchronize unreleased free predictions in the same channel.
